About

Financial Fraud Detective is a free educational game from WGRALGO / The Wealth Gap Resolution Algorithm™ Inc. It helps you practice spotting the money traps that go beyond everyday scam texts: identity theft, car deals, contractors, loans, taxes, benefits, and small-business schemes.

Real fraud schemes change all the time, and these cases are illustrative patterns. Always check a suspicious message through the official channel of the company or agency it claims to be from. Where real companies or agencies are named, it’s only as plain educational text.

Financial Fraud Detective v1.1.0. For education and awareness only. Not legal, financial, banking, cybersecurity, or fraud-investigation advice.

Privacy

This app works offline. It does not collect personal data, and all gameplay stays on your device. Your best score is kept only while the app is open.

  • No ads
  • No account
  • No analytics or trackers
  • No subscription
  • No cloud sync or backend server
  • No INTERNET permission
